About Us

Our Background

ChoiceVishwa was founded in 2020 with a vision to simplify the college admission process for students in India. We noticed that thousands of MHT CET candidates struggled to make the right college decision due to a lack of transparent, data-driven insights. Our platform is built on advanced research that analyze past cutoff trends, student preferences, and admission criteria to generate the best possible college preference list for you.

Our Vision

To become India’s most trusted college admission guidance platform, where every student can make informed decisions with clarity and confidence. We strive to eliminate confusion, stress, and misinformation in the admission process.

Our Mission

- Empower students with accurate and transparent data.

- Simplify college selection through research-driven insights.

- Ensure stress-free admissions by guiding students every step of the way.

- Promote accessibility by providing affordable solutions for every student.
